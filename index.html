Critical Rule Comparison Matrix

Grammar Pattern Governing Subject Verb Form Exam Exemplar
Additive Connectors
(as well as, along with, together with, with)		 Subject 1 (First subject) Follows S1 strictly The captain as well as his players was awarded.
Correlative Conjunctions
(either... or, neither... nor, not only... but also)		 Subject 2 (Nearest subject) Follows S2 strictly Neither the manager nor his assistants were present.
Isolated Pronouns
(Neither of, Either of, Each of, Everyone)		 Grammatically Singular Always Singular Neither of the two solutions is viable.
Quantifier: A number of Indefinite Plural Collection Always Plural A number of students have registered.
Statistic: The number of Definite Numerical Statistic Always Singular The number of students is seventy-five.
Quantities of Money, Time, Distance Single Unified Unit Always Singular Fifty thousand dollars was invested.

Causative Quick Comparison Matrix

Verb Active Voice Formula Passive Voice Formula Nuance & Meaning
HAVE S + have + Person + V1 (bare) S + have + Thing + V3 Delegation, formal assignment, instruction.
GET S + get + Person + TO + V1 S + get + Thing + V3 Persuasion, convincing, overcoming resistance.
MAKE S + make + Person + V1 (bare) S + BE MADE + TO + V1 (Trap!) Force, compulsion, unavoidable obligation.
LET S + let + Person + V1 (bare) S + BE ALLOWED TO + V1 Permission, authorization, non-prevention.
HELP S + help + Person + (TO) + V1 S + BE HELPED + TO + V1 Assistance (both bare and to-infinitive correct).

About EngMaster IIUC & Course GEEL-1106

Course specification, institutional framework, and offline technical architecture.

Course Overview & Departmental Affiliation

Course Code: GEEL-1106 / UREL-1106
Course Title: Advanced English
Academic Credits: 2.0 Credit Hours (Graded)
Administering Body: Center for General Education (CGED), International Islamic University Chittagong (IIUC), Kumira, Sitakunda, Chattogram-4318, Bangladesh.
Target Cohort: First Year First Semester (Autumn & Spring sessions) across the Department of Computer Science & Engineering (CSE), Department of Electrical & Electronic Engineering (EEE), and Department of Civil Engineering (CEE).

Offline-First Zero-Dependency Architecture

EngMaster IIUC has been engineered as a single-page, 100% offline educational web application. It requires zero backend servers, zero databases, zero Node.js runtimes, and zero external build tools. Once index.html is opened in any modern browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari), all 16 textbook modules, 100 grammar rules, 50 mistakes, 50 vocabulary entries, 7 years of previous exam questions, and the 45-minute mock test simulator execute entirely in client-side memory with instant responsiveness.

Audio Pronunciation Technology

Interactive vocabulary pronunciation is powered entirely by the native browser Web Speech API (window.speechSynthesis), requiring zero external audio file downloads or active internet connections.
