# EngMaster IIUC — GEEL-1106 / UREL-1106 Academic Learning Platform (LMS)

> **Official Interactive Learning & Examination Platform for Course Code: GEEL-1106 / UREL-1106: Advanced English**  
> Administered by the **Center for General Education (CGED)**, International Islamic University Chittagong (IIUC).

---

## 🌟 Overview & Educational Architecture

**EngMaster IIUC** transforms the entire first-year undergraduate English curriculum into a high-performance, single-page, **100% offline-first interactive Learning Management System (LMS)**. 

Rather than functioning as a static PDF viewer, the platform intelligently organizes all syllabus components, prescribed textbooks (E.L. Tibbitts' *Exercises in Reading Comprehension*, A.J. Glover's *Build Up Your English*, John Bunyan's *The Pilgrim's Progress*, Ernest Hemingway's *The Old Man and the Sea*), grammar lecture slide decks, and seven years of solved Semester End Examinations (2018–2025) into searchable, interactive, and academically rigorous modules.

> [!NOTE]
> **Strict Syllabus Constraint:** In accordance with course guidelines, this platform is completely visual and interactive with **zero audio or listening dependencies**.

## 🏛️ Course Structure & Curricular Hierarchy

### 1. 🏠 Command Center & Home Dashboard
- **Syllabus Mastery Tracker:** Real-time percentage indicator tracking completed modules, mastered grammar rules, and reading progress.
- **Continue Learning:** One-click resumption of the student's most recently viewed chapter or practice drill.
- **Daily Study Goals & Streak Counter:** Dynamic daily target selector (e.g. 20 Rules, 15 MCQs) with persistent `localStorage` streak tracking.
- **Live Exam Countdown:** Configurable real-time countdown timer to both Midterm and Final Semester End Examinations.
- **Recent Activity Feed:** Automated chronological log of user revisions, quizzes, bookmarked rules, and personal notes.
- **Quick Revision Launcher:** Instant access cards to Top 100 Rules, Top 50 Common Mistakes, and the 15-Point Sanity Checklist.

### 2. 📚 Midterm Hub (CIE 50 Marks / Midterm 30 Marks)
- **Section 1 — Prescribed Reading:**
  - *Exercises in Reading Comprehension (Tibbitts Units 1C–10C)*: Introduction, Summary, Essential Vocabulary, Questions, Model Answers, Practice MCQ/CQ.
  - *Build Up Your English (A.J. Glover)*: Structural synthesis, adverbial particles, and phrase completion drills.
  - *The Pilgrim's Progress (John Bunyan)*: Allegorical summary, key themes (Faith, Temptation, Perseverance), character profiles (Christian, Faithful, Hopeful, Giant Despair), university exam questions and model answers.
  - *The Old Man and the Sea (Ernest Hemingway)*: Novella summary, themes (Human Endurance, Dignity in Defeat, Man vs Nature), character analysis (Santiago, Manolin, The Marlin, The Sharks), exam questions and solved CQ answers.
- **Section 2 — Reading Skills & Strategies:**
  - 12 In-depth modules: Scanning, Skimming, Identifying Main Ideas, Supporting Details, Summarizing, Inferring Opinion & Attitude, Understanding Argument Structures, Inference, Text Analysis, Literary vs. Non-Literary Practice, and Speed Reading Techniques (with words-per-minute calculator).
- **Section 3 — Writing & Communication:**
  - *Sentence Types*: Assertive, Imperative, Interrogative, Optative, Exclamatory; structural forms (Simple, Complex, Compound) with transformation rules and formulas.
  - *Grammar Focus*: Active & Passive Voice conversion formulas, Sequence of Tense matrix, WH-Questions formulation, Yes/No Question inversion rules.
  - *Writing Studio*: Paragraph Writing (Guided Paragraphs, Free Paragraphs); Story Writing & Story Completion frameworks.
  - *Oral Communication & Dialogue*: Professional Self-Introduction for engineering students, Situational Dialogue Writing scripts.

### 3. 🎓 Final Exam Hub (Semester End Examination — 50 Marks)
- **Section 1 — Prescribed Reading & Literary Analysis:**
  - *Tibbitts Units 11C–20C*: Full analysis of *The Path of Power (The Film Director)*, *The Co-operative Movement*, *The French Revolution & Third Estate*, *The Birth of the UNO*, *Mass Production*, with full passages, vocabulary breakdown, inferential questions, and 50-word precision summaries.
  - *The Pilgrim's Progress & The Old Man and the Sea (Finals Focus)*: Advanced critical analysis, symbolic motifs, major quotes, model CQ answers for Semester End Examinations.
- **Section 2 — Reading & Comprehension:**
  - Real-world article studies from *Dhaka Courier*, contemporary international dailies, and IELTS Academic Reading passages.
  - Reading skills application: Extracting authorial tone, mood, bias, argumentative reasoning, and drafting strict 45–50 word summaries.
- **Section 3 — Core Grammar Mastery Chapters:**
  - 7 Master Chapters:
    1. **Tenses & Right Forms of Verbs**
    2. **Articles & Determiners**
    3. **Prepositions, Connectors & Conjunctions**
    4. **Subject–Verb Agreement (Grammatical Concord)**
    5. **Clauses & Sentence Synthesis**
    6. **Modals & Semi-Modals**
    7. **Conditional Sentences (Real & Unreal)**
  - Every chapter strictly formatted as: `Definition` &rarr; `Rules` &rarr; `Formula` &rarr; `Structure` &rarr; `Examples` &rarr; `Wrong vs Correct Usage` &rarr; `Exceptions` &rarr; `Common Mistakes` &rarr; `Shortcut Tricks` &rarr; `Practice (MCQ & CQ)` &rarr; `Previous Exam Questions` &rarr; `Revision Notes`.
- **Section 4 — Professional Composition & Technical Writing:**
  - *Graph & Chart Description*: Pie Charts, Bar Charts, Column Charts, Line Graphs, Data Tables. (Vocabulary of trends, 3-paragraph format, model university exam answers).
  - *Opinion & Argumentative Writing*: Agree/Disagree framework, presenting counter-arguments, thesis statement development, transitional linkers.
  - *Business & Official Correspondence*: Formal Letters (Order, Complaint, Request, Inquiry, Credit/Collection) and Informal Letters. Structure, format templates, editable practice pad, and examiner grading rubric.
- **Section 5 — Academic Speaking & Presentation:**
  - Describing People & Personalities, Describing Places & Environments, Narrating Events & Technical Processes, Extempore Speech strategies (PREP method), and Professional Presentation Techniques.
- **Section 6 — Vocabulary Master Catalog:**
  - Categorized masterlists: Important Academic Words, Difficult Vocabulary, Synonyms & Antonyms, Essential Idioms, Verb Phrases, Academic Collocations, and High-Yield Examination Vocabulary.

### 4. 🏛️ Previous Question Bank (2018–2025)
- Comprehensive archive covering Spring and Autumn semesters from 2018 through 2025.
- Multi-dimensional filtering:
  - By **Year** (2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025)
  - By **Semester** (Spring / Autumn)
  - By **Topic** (SVA, Conditionals, Causatives, Prepositions, Voice, Correction, Seen Passage, Unseen Summary, Graph, Letter)
  - By **Question Type** (Fill in the Blanks, Sentence Correction, Rewrite/Synthesis, Short Answer, MCQ, Essay)
- Features: Official verified model answer reveal, grammatical rationale, difficulty level rating (Easy, Medium, Challenging), and a **Frequently Asked Indicator** highlighting recurring question types.

### 5. ⚡ Practice Zone (9 Interactive Drill Modes)
- **MCQ Practice:** Multiple choice questions with immediate evaluation and score tracking.
- **CQ Analytical Practice:** Analytical comprehension questions with official Bloom's Taxonomy grading rubrics.
- **Grammar Drills:** Topic-specific grammar exercises (Voice, Sequence of Tense, Modals).
- **Reading Drills:** Evidence extraction exercises from unseen and seen passages.
- **Writing Drills:** Precision drafting and summary exercises with word count benchmarks.
- **Sentence Correction Drills:** Spotting and fixing authentic university exam errors with detailed rationale.
- **Fill in the Blanks:** Prepositions, conjunctions, and right forms of verbs with hint toggles.
- **Error Identification:** Sentence diagnostics with clickable error token identification.
- **Mixed Practice Arena:** Randomized multi-topic practice sessions.

### 6. 🚀 Exam Preparation & Cram Booster
- **Exam Suggestions & Priority Matrix:** Category A (100% Recurrence), Category B (70–85%), Category C (40–60%), and Top 10 University Error Traps.
- **Last-Night Revision & Sanity Checklist:** 15-point essential checklist with interactive tickboxes and persistent state.
- **Formula Sheets & Grammar Cheat Sheets:** One-page summary tables for tenses, conditionals, causatives, voice, and concord.
- **Top 100 Rules Catalog:** Searchable cards with mastery toggles and bookmarking.
- **Top 50 Common Mistakes:** Side-by-side incorrect vs. correct comparisons with grammatical justifications.

### 7. ⏱️ Multi-Track Mock Exam Suite
6 Dedicated examination simulations:
1. **Grammar Master Mock Test** (25 Questions | 30 Minutes | Negative marking toggle)
2. **Reading Comprehension Mock Test** (1 Passage + 15 Questions | 35 Minutes)
3. **Professional Writing Mock Workshop** (Letter + Essay drafting | 40 Minutes | Word count evaluation)
4. **Academic Vocabulary Sprint** (30 Questions | 20 Minutes)
5. **Mixed Midterm Mock Test** (25 Questions | 45 Minutes | CIE Syllabus)
6. **Full Final Semester End Mock Examination** (45 Questions | 60 Minutes)
- **Features across all tests:** Real-time countdown timer, question palette grid with jump-to-question buttons, answered/unanswered counters, pause/resume controls, auto-submit on timeout, and a comprehensive **Diagnostic Performance Report** (letter grades A+ through F, percentage scores, topic weakness analysis, and targeted revision recommendations).

### 8. 👤 Personal Features & Global Search
- **Unified Global Search:** High-speed real-time search indexing all Grammar Rules, Vocabulary, Reading Passages, Composition Templates, Previous Questions, MCQs, and Smart Notes simultaneously.
- **Personal Notebook (Smart Notes):** Create, categorize, edit, and delete personal lecture notes and study reminders stored in `localStorage`.
- **Bookmark Hub:** Instant saving and retrieval of rules, vocabulary words, and past questions.
- **Quiz & Test History:** Persistent record of past mock test scores, completion dates, and grades.
- **Theme & Typography Controls:** Seamless Dark Mode / Academic Light Mode toggle, dynamic font-size adjuster (Standard, Large, Extra Large), and print-optimized styling.

## 💻 Technology Stack

| Technology | Purpose & Implementation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **HTML5** | Semantic, accessible structure with dedicated view containers. |
| **Modern CSS3** | Custom properties (CSS variables), glassmorphism (`backdrop-filter`), fluid flexbox/grid layout (responsive for mobile, tablet, and 100% widescreen displays), and clean `@media print` rules. |
| **Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+)** | Single-page client-side router, search engine, state management, quiz evaluation, and markdown rendering. |
| **LocalStorage API** | Complete client-side state persistence for bookmarks, notes, quiz scores, daily goals, and visual preferences. |

> **No Backend • No Database • No Node.js • No React/Vue • No Build Tools Required.**

## 🏃 How to Run Locally

1. Open the `website/` folder on your computer.
2. Double-click **`index.html`** or right-click and open with any modern web browser:
   - Google Chrome
   - Microsoft Edge
   - Mozilla Firefox
   - Apple Safari
   - Brave / Opera

No server setup or internet connection is required.
